The Geometry of Desert Light: How Greig Fraser Framed Arrakis

ES
Elena S. • Head Archivist

When Denis Villeneuve and cinematographer Greig Fraser set out to capture the harsh dunes of Arrakis for Dune: Part Two, their primary objective was to avoid the sterile sheen of modern CGI sandscapes. Instead, they developed a pioneering hybrid photochemical pipeline.

Fraser utilized custom large-format digital sensors adapted with 1980s Soviet spherical optics and un-coated German glass. The digital negative was then laser-recorded onto real 35mm motion picture film stock before being scanned back to 4K digital for final theatrical distribution.
